Vision

To produce God-centered graduates, value-driven servant leaders who are globally competent IT professionals, passionate, capable of solving problems and contributing to society through innovation and leadership in the 21st century.

Mission

To equip students with knowledge, problem-solving skills, creativity, and competencies in adaptability, interdisciplinary approach, and industry relevance — becoming proficient IT professionals capable of addressing real-world challenges through technology.

The Information Technology department is committed to:

1. Ensure quality teaching for the entire development of computer experts who are dedicated and competent in meeting the demands of the global community. 2. Produce skilled, productive and dynamic leaders who will contribute to the physical, spiritual, moral, economic development of the local, national and global community. 3. Develop individuals capable of bringing transformation and make an impact to the world in an innovative way.

Program Goals

1. Prepare and equip students to be professionals in their field. 2. Train students in abstract and analytical processes. 3. Provide opportunities for practical experience in system installation, management, and administration. 4. Develop students’ ability to conceptualize, design, develop, and maintain information systems.

Program Objectives

1. Teach interpretation, evaluation, and promotion of information and knowledge resources. 2. Provide quality advising that promotes informed program choice. 3. Model professionalism, ethics, and access to information in a democratic society. 4. Apply technology productively in instruction and research. 5. Professional attitudes regarding scholarship, professional ethics, intellectual freedom, and access to information in a diverse democratic society. 6. Assist students to undergo training in abstract and analytical processes. 7. Provide adequate opportunities for students to acquire practical knowledge of how information systems are installed, operated, managed and administered. 8. Model the practical and productive use of information technology in instruction.

MEET BSIT OFFICERS SY 2025-2026

The BSIT Officer is the student's main helper for all things related to the IT course. They are chosen by their peers to speak up for them and share their ideas or concerns with the teachers and the school's leaders. This officers work as the go-between, making sure student needs are heard and problems get fixed quickly. They also take charge of planning fun events, like tech workshops, coding contests, and social gatherings to help students learn and meet each other. Their main job is to build a friendly community and make sure all IT students are dedicated to finish this field. Ultimately, they aim to ensure students have the support and tools they need to succeed in their studies.

MEET THE SYNTAX ORGANIZATION OFFICERS

The Syntax Organization is the official student group for all IT students, led by a dedicated Teacher Adviser and elected Student Officers. This group is not just for social events; it's organized into specialized teams to cover different areas of student life and technology. For instance, CIMUS focuses on organizing sports and recreational activities to promote student wellness and teamwork. The STAC Technical Team handles the setup and support for all technology needed during organization events and school programs. Meanwhile, HEXHIVE Programming provides a space for students passionate about coding to work on projects and participate in programming contests. Finally, the CSS Multimedia team manages all content creation, including photos, videos, and graphics, to document and promote the organization's activities. Together, these different fields ensure the Syntax Organization supports every IT student's academic, technical, and social needs.

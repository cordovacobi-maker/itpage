Meet Our Program Head and Advisers

Bachelor of Science and Information Technology Instructors at Davao Vision Colleges Inc. are the personnel responsible for the management, maintenance, and security of the college's entire technological infrastructure. They ensure all computer labs, classroom technology, administrative systems, and network services are operational and provide technical support to students, faculty, and administration. IT Facilities at Davao Vision Colleges Inc. refer to the physical spaces and equipment that support the school's technology. This includes computer laboratories for student use, the central server room where core network and data systems are housed, and the necessary network cabling and hardware that provides internet and connectivity across the campus.
